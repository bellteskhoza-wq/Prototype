Welcome to Neelo's

A place where great service meets good vibes.

At Neelo's, we believe that looking after your vehicle should feel like a treat. Our team is here to give every car attentive care, from a quick exterior wash to a more detailed clean that brings back that just-detailed finish.

And when it is time to take a break, our shisanyama-inspired social space gives friends and family a place to connect, enjoy good food and make the most of the day.

We aim to make every visit welcoming, convenient and worth coming back for. Pull in, put your feet up and let us take care of the rest.
